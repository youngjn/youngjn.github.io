FAQ

What is keeep?

keeep is a simple link archive for Safari. Save links and keep them organized — no more cluttered tabs or lost pages.

How much does keeep cost?

keeep is completely free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

How do I save items?

Use the built-in Share Extension in Safari. Tap the share button on any product page and select keeep to save it instantly.

Does keeep collect my data?

No. All your saved items are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit any personal data.

Can I view saved products without leaving the app?

Yes. Tap any card to open the product page in keeep's built-in browser.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 23, 2026

At keeep, we believe that your data belongs to you. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our wishlist management services, including our Safari Share Extension.

1. Information Collection and Use

Local Data Storage

keeep is designed with a "privacy-first" architecture. All items you save—including URLs, product titles, and images—are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

  • App Groups: We use App Groups to allow the Safari Share Extension to securely pass URLs to the main app container. This data remains on your physical device.
  • CloudKit (Optional): If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your wishlist data may be synced across your own devices via Apple's iCloud service. We do not have access to this data; it is encrypted and managed by Apple.

Metadata Extraction

When you save a URL, the app uses the LPMetadataProvider API to fetch a preview (title and image) of the website.

  • This request is made directly from your device to the website's server.
  • keeep does not route these requests through any third-party servers or our own servers.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

However, because keeep allows you to browse saved links via an in-app browser (SFSafariViewController), the websites you visit may collect their own data (cookies, IP addresses, etc.) as if you were browsing in Safari. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the websites you save to your wishlist.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data.

  • You can delete individual items from your wishlist at any time using the swipe-to-delete gesture.
  • Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data associated with keeep unless it has been synced to your personal iCloud storage.

4. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Since all data is stored locally on your device, we have no way of knowing who is using the app.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app or on our official distribution page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at zero.june@outlook.com.
